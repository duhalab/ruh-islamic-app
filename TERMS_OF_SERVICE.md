### B. Prayer Time Calculations
Prayer times are determined using established astronomical algorithms and calculation standards (including Islamic Foundation Bangladesh, Karachi University, Muslim World League, Umm al-Qura, and ISNA). Local prayer times in specific mosques may vary slightly based on congregation schedules, elevation, or atmospheric conditions. Users are advised to exercise religious caution (*Ihtiyat*).

### C. Qibla Compass Accuracy
The compass bearing to the Holy Kaaba is computed using your device's built-in magnetometer (compass sensor) and geographical coordinates. Sensor readings can be affected by physical interference (such as magnetic phone cases, metal objects, or nearby electronics). Users should calibrate their device sensor (by rotating the phone in a figure-8 motion) prior to checking the Qibla.

### D. Zakat Calculator
The Zakat Calculator provides general estimates based on standard Nisab thresholds. For complex financial holdings, shared business partnerships, or intricate estate planning, consultation with a qualified Islamic scholar or Mufti is advised.
